# Source and asset provenance

This release contains original project source and documentation, synthetic tests, and an agent workflow skill. General methods were developed while iterating on 2D sprite production: physical limb ownership, source-connected shoulders, complete clothing surfaces, support-exchange continuity, scoped repairs, and honest temporal evidence.

No client/game character artwork, original uploads, project-specific registrations, private names, correspondence, delivery records, credentials, or prior project history is included. Character-specific production scripts were not copied into the public examples. Reusable algorithms require explicit authored inputs rather than hidden example coordinates.

`demo.py`, `synthetic_profiles.py` and the layered demo construct new simple geometric diagrams using drawing primitives. Their source and generated diagrams may be used under the repository's MIT license. They are technical fixtures, not artistic acceptance examples. No imported images are committed in this initial source release.

External artwork remains the responsibility of its owner. Importing an image, writing a provider request, or rendering a sprite does not confer copyright or waive provider conditions. Keep inferred hidden surfaces and source edits distinguishable from untouched original pixels. Record references and permissions in the private production workspace, not this public repository.

Dependencies are not bundled; their licenses are described in `DEPENDENCIES.md`. This document describes the curated release scope, not a legal opinion or exhaustive originality guarantee.

The authored dash repair helpers and their demonstration also use only parameterized algorithms and newly code-drawn shapes. No production landmark arrays, character identities, generation sheets, accepted media or upload records are included. The local cubic interpolation is an original implementation of the standard shape-preserving Hermite method, not vendored SciPy source.

Pose/hand review contracts preserve general lessons about independent surface, thumb-side, fist-direction and attachment checks. Review fixtures use newly constructed geometric shapes and authored annotations, not production images or measurements. Worksheet validation and geometric diagnostics cannot establish anatomical correctness or prove that a reviewer watched playback.

Continuity-repair diagnostics and tests use original code-drawn rectangles and deliberately introduced pixel defects under the same MIT license. They generalize whole-component coverage, explicit foreground ownership, integer-shifted opaque cores and local backing preservation. No production scripts, character artwork, names, coordinates, generated assets or delivery records are copied into these fixtures.
